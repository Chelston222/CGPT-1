# 222Emails Proof Capture and Case Study OS

**Purpose:** Turn real delivery into permission-cleared, defensible proof without inflating attribution.

## 1. Why this exists

222Emails' strongest direct competitors currently have more publishable proof. The response is not to imitate their numbers. The response is to make proof capture part of delivery so every suitable engagement has the chance to become a trustworthy case study.

## 2. Proof states

Every evidence item must be labelled internally as one of:

- **VERIFIED:** directly supported by source data or documented delivery evidence.
- **CLIENT-REPORTED:** supplied by the client but not independently reconciled by 222Emails.
- **ESTIMATED:** calculated from stated assumptions and labelled as such.
- **ILLUSTRATIVE:** example only, not live client data.
- **UNKNOWN:** evidence is not available or cannot be supported.

Do not silently upgrade one state into another.

## 3. Create a proof record at the start of every paid engagement

Record before implementation:

### Client identity
- company name;
- vertical;
- location;
- primary contact;
- permission status: private / anonymised / named case study / quote approved;
- approval date and approver.

### Baseline
- date baseline captured;
- systems in use;
- current journey state;
- exact leak being addressed;
- eligible customer / enquiry population if known;
- baseline bookings / rebookings / replies / no-shows / lapsed count where relevant and reliable;
- existing automation state;
- material data-quality limitations;
- known external factors.

### Commercial context
- average booking value if supplied and relevant;
- margin only if supplied and necessary;
- service capacity constraints;
- discounts/offers used;
- advertising/acquisition changes happening at the same time;
- seasonality or events that could distort comparison.

## 4. Scope record

Before launch, capture exactly what 222Emails changed:

- journey / asset name;
- trigger;
- customer eligibility;
- timing rule source;
- messages / channels;
- stop conditions;
- suppression logic;
- human handoff;
- data fields or integration used;
- QA tests;
- measurement events;
- owner after handover.

Also record what 222Emails did **not** change. This matters for attribution.

## 5. Launch record

- planned launch date;
- actual launch date;
- cause of any delay;
- access/readiness/approval dependencies;
- test contacts / test events completed;
- defects found pre-launch;
- defects found post-launch;
- final sign-off date.

This creates on-time implementation proof without pretending client-caused delays were 222Emails delivery time.

## 6. Event-backed outcome capture

Where systems permit, capture event-level evidence rather than dashboard screenshots alone.

Potential events:
- eligible customer entered journey;
- message/action triggered;
- delivery confirmed where available;
- customer replied;
- customer clicked booking route;
- appointment booked;
- appointment attended;
- customer rebooked;
- customer opted out;
- staff handoff occurred;
- journey stopped correctly.

Never interpret “message sent” as recovered revenue.

## 7. Commercial outcome reconciliation

For any revenue claim, answer:

1. What transaction / booking event supports it?
2. Was it completed or merely booked?
3. Gross revenue or net revenue?
4. Was a discount used?
5. Was the customer exposed to other campaigns at the same time?
6. Could the booking have happened without the system?
7. What attribution window is being used and why?
8. Is the number platform-attributed, client-reported or independently reconciled?

If those questions cannot be answered, downgrade the claim instead of guessing.

## 8. Case-study publishing threshold

A result can become a public case study only when:

- client permission is explicit;
- scope is documented;
- baseline is usable or the lack of one is disclosed;
- outcome evidence is supportable;
- attribution boundary is stated;
- no confidential or personal customer data is exposed;
- screenshots are redacted appropriately;
- client quote is approved if used;
- no medical, financial or other regulated claim is introduced beyond evidence.

## 9. Public case-study structure

### Headline
Describe the verified commercial or operating change, not a hype claim.

### 1. Business context
What kind of appointment-led business was involved and what was the relevant customer journey?

### 2. The leak
What was happening before the build?

### 3. Evidence at baseline
What could actually be observed?

### 4. The Client Return System
What did 222Emails install or change?

### 5. What stayed unchanged
Name major factors 222Emails did not control.

### 6. Implementation
Show timing, dependencies and QA.

### 7. Verified outcome
Show only supported metrics.

### 8. Attribution boundary
Explain what the evidence can and cannot prove.

### 9. Client voice
Approved quote only.

### 10. Visual proof
Use redacted journey map, workflow screenshot, QA record or event trail. Label illustrative visuals as illustrative.

### 11. CTA
Free Revenue Recovery Check.

## 10. Proof asset ladder

One engagement can create multiple legitimate assets:

1. **Delivery proof:** system map / QA / handover.
2. **Process proof:** how the leak was diagnosed.
3. **Operating proof:** trigger, stop and handoff worked as designed.
4. **Outcome proof:** replies, bookings, rebookings or other verified events.
5. **Commercial proof:** revenue only when supportable.
6. **Client proof:** approved testimonial or quote.
7. **Authority content:** anonymised lesson from the engagement.

Do not wait for a giant revenue number before publishing useful proof.

## 11. Minimum competitive proof target

Before claiming the appointment-led model is fully proven, target:

- three paid engagements;
- at least one paid £997 Sprint;
- three permission-cleared appointment-led proof assets;
- at least one completed implementation with scope and on-time evidence;
- event-backed metric integrity;
- safe sender / consent / suppression behaviour.

## 12. Testimonial request

After the agreed outcome / handover, ask for an honest statement covering:

- what problem they had before;
- what 222Emails changed;
- what became easier / clearer / better;
- any result they personally feel comfortable verifying;
- what working with 222Emails was like.

Do not write a glowing result and ask the client merely to approve it. Drafting for clarity is fine only after the client has supplied the substance and has final approval.

## 13. Public results hub once threshold is met

Create `/results/` only when there is enough real material to make it useful.

Suggested filters:
- salons & barbers;
- aesthetics / skin clinics;
- dental;
- enquiry recovery;
- rebooking;
- reactivation;
- no-show recovery;
- operating / QA proof.

Each case should internally link to the relevant service/vertical page and the Revenue Recovery Check.

## 14. Competitive standard

Stimulate wins with volume of quantified cases. Aura Direct wins with dramatic reactivation outcomes. Blackburn Digital wins with founder/client credibility. Everboost wins with sophisticated commercial measurement.

222Emails should aim to win on **evidence clarity**:

> what was true before, what we changed, what happened after, and exactly how certain we are that those things are connected.
