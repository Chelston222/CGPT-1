# 222Emails Content Revenue Flywheel

## Objective

Turn useful commercial signals into content once, distribute only reviewed revisions, learn from verified outcomes, and multiply evidence-backed winners without weakening publishing authority.

## Flow

**Idea → Build → Approve → Publish → Learn → Multiply → Revenue**

### Idea
Capture the customer problem, desired outcome, mechanism, audience and evidence level in the Notion Content Intelligence Bank.

### Build
Create one or more platform-specific outputs from the idea. Each output keeps a relation to the source idea. A generated image, caption or performance score is not publication permission.

### Approve
The content row must clear factual, brand, source, identity, freshness and exact-media checks. The Notion reserve bridge additionally requires an explicit Approved state and Approved for Publish before staging.

### Publish
A staged reserve item remains review-only. The existing GitHub owner-approval contract, capacity controls, durable dispatch intent, Buffer acceptance ledger and publication verifier remain authoritative.

### Learn
Performance Learning V2 uses only mature, independently verified evidence. It protects against small samples, stale evidence and content fatigue, and keeps deliberate exploration in the mix.

### Multiply
A proven idea may create derivatives, but each derivative becomes a new reviewed output. A winner does not grant automatic publishing permission to its derivatives.

### Revenue
Commercial outcomes are recorded only when defensibly attributable. Qualified conversations and paid progression may outrank vanity engagement in learning, but missing commercial evidence must never be fabricated.

## Owner experience

The intended operating surface is five buckets: **Ideas, Build, Ready, Live, Winners**. Technical evidence fields remain underneath for reliability rather than becoming routine owner admin.

## Zero-touch claim gate

Do not call the system zero-touch until a real approved single-image reserve item has completed the entire handoff through staging, separate owner approval, Buffer acceptance, publication verification and evidence writeback without duplicate creation.
