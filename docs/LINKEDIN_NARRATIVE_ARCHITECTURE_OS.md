# LinkedIn Narrative Architecture OS v1.0

Effective: 20 September 2026
Status: CURRENT CANONICAL NARRATIVE-SELECTION LAYER
Parent: `LINKEDIN_STORY_WORTH_READING_STANDARD.md`

## Purpose

This layer chooses the most appropriate narrative structure only **after** source truth and Story Worth Reading have established that there is something real worth saying.

Frameworks are tools. They are never the story.

## Canonical sequence

**SOURCE TRUTH → STORY WORTH READING → CONTENT JOB → NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE → HOOK OS → VOICE / SLOPMONSTER → PROOF / COMMERCIAL TRUTH → OWNER APPROVAL → BUFFER → VERIFIED PERFORMANCE LEARNING**

If any downstream layer conflicts with source truth or Story Worth Reading, the downstream layer loses.

## Source-truth precondition

Before choosing a framework, answer:

1. What actually happened?
2. What was at stake?
3. What changed, failed, conflicted or remains unproven?
4. Which details are verified rather than inferred?
5. What does the reader need to understand that cannot be seen from the image alone?

If one missing fact is the difference between a generic caption and a real story, ask Chelston one precise question. Do not invent the missing scene, emotion, number, chronology or outcome.

## Content-job classifier

Choose the job before choosing the structure:

- **founder_story**: reveal a real moment, tension or change;
- **operator_build_in_public**: document live work, numbers, decisions and what remains unproven;
- **teaching**: explain a mechanism or method the reader can use;
- **diagnostic**: help a buyer recognise a problem or leak;
- **observation_pov**: make a reasoned, distinctive observation;
- **proof_outcome**: show a defensible result, experiment or progression with limits;
- **offer_route**: make the next commercial step clear without manufacturing urgency.

A post can have a secondary job, but it needs one primary job.

## Narrative Architecture Library

### 1. RAW STORY

Use when the human event is the value.

**Event → Scene → Friction → What happened → Current truth → Earned ending**

No lesson, tips or question are required.

Best for: personal moments, uncomfortable admissions, decisions, relationship between ambition and current reality, unresolved founder experiences.

### 2. SLAY OVERLAY

Source: Lara Acosta's public SLAY framework.

Original public framework:
**Story → Lesson → Actionable Advice → You**

Lara's public explanations commonly describe opening with a story, moving to a lesson, adding practical advice, then turning the post back to the reader, often with a question. She has also publicly described thinking about the lesson first and the story second as an ideation method.

222E adaptation:
- keep Lara's framework attribution explicit in internal documentation;
- use SLAY only when a real story genuinely earns a useful lesson and practical action;
- **Y / You** may be reader relevance, application or handoff; a question is optional;
- do not misrepresent this adaptation as Lara's exact original rule;
- never force actionable advice, a listicle or a comment-bait question.

SLAY is ineligible when the truthful ending is unresolved, the story has no practical lesson, or the lesson would require fake hindsight.

### 3. CONTRAST / GAP

**Visible or expected outcome → Current reality → Gap → Work underway → What remains unproven**

Best for: aspirational imagery, revenue targets, future ambitions, before/after expectation gaps.

This structure must reduce status-performance, not increase it.

### 4. FAILURE / CORRECTION

**Belief or plan → What happened → Where it broke → Consequence → Change now → Open proof**

Best for: bad assumptions, failed offers, missed targets, overbuilt systems, process corrections.

Do not create a heroic redemption ending unless the evidence exists.

### 5. OPERATOR / BUILD-IN-PUBLIC

**Live number or event → Actions taken → What moved → What did not → Next proof point**

Best for: calls, sales process, revenue weeks, experiments, systems and team handoffs.

One good result must not be written as repeatability. Current truth is more important than confidence theatre.

### 6. TEACHING / DIAGNOSTIC

**Problem → Mechanism → Practical application → Boundary / next step**

Best for: buyer education, Revenue Recovery, Client Return Systems, Retention School and operational teaching.

A story is optional. Do not invent a story merely to humanise educational content.

### 7. OBSERVATION / POV

**Observed pattern → Clear stance → Evidence / reasoning → Implication**

Best for: market commentary, operator beliefs and useful disagreement.

Contrarianism must be earned by reasoning or evidence. Fake contrarian hooks are rejected.

## Kleo research protocol

Kleo's public personal-brand guidance recommends studying a small group of relevant creators, identifying posts that materially outperform their normal content, then analysing:

- how the post opens;
- how the body is organised;
- how it closes;
- why it connects;
- which structural choices can be transferred without copying content.

Kleo is therefore used as a **research-method benchmark**, not as a canonical writing authority.

## Mobile-hook heuristic

Lara has publicly described an 8–10 word mobile-hook preference.

Treat this as a **heuristic**, never a hard gate.

Shorten when it improves clarity and tension.
Keep a longer hook when shortening would damage truth, specificity, voice or the natural sentence.

The first visible lines must pay off in the next paragraph. No clickbait that the body cannot cash.

## Lesson-first safeguard

A lesson can help select which real story is worth telling.

It cannot be used to:
- manufacture a story;
- distort chronology;
- cherry-pick facts until they prove the lesson;
- convert an unresolved event into fake certainty;
- invent a win;
- force a moral where none exists.

Before drafting, always return to: **what actually happened?**

## Anti-template lock

The system must not converge on one recognisable LinkedIn shape.

Treat repetition of these as fatigue:
- same hook family;
- same two-line contradiction rhythm;
- same paragraph length pattern;
- same three-point list;
- same lesson placement;
- same question ending;
- same emotional arc;
- same “I was wrong” confession pattern.

A post may break every listed architecture when the truthful version reads better.

## Reader handoff rule

A final question is not mandatory.

Use a question only when:
- the reader can answer something meaningful;
- the answer advances discussion;
- the question is not obvious engagement bait;
- the post has already delivered value.

Alternative handoffs include:
- a direct practical instruction;
- an invitation to compare;
- a clear implication;
- an unresolved current truth;
- no handoff at all.

## Performance hierarchy

Optimise narrative structures against:
1. attributable commercial progression;
2. qualified conversations and Revenue Recovery Check progression;
3. relevant DMs and target-role response;
4. saves, meaningful shares and profile intent where available;
5. reach and generic engagement as diagnostic context.

Do not declare a structure a winner from one post.

Do not let a high-performing structure rescue weak source truth or generic writing.

## Release gate

Before a personal founder / photo-led post can be approved:

1. Source truth is complete, or one precise question has been asked.
2. Story Worth Reading passes.
3. Primary content job is identified.
4. Architecture is selected only if it improves fit.
5. Hook OS passes.
6. No creator wording, cadence or persona has been copied.
7. No forced lesson, list, CTA or engagement question exists.
8. No borrowed status, envy bait or fake vulnerability exists.
9. SlopMonster + Chelston Originality Gate passes.
10. Proof / commercial truth passes.
11. Owner approval remains explicit.
