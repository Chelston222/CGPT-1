# 222Emails Hook Operating System v1.0

Effective date: 2026-08-30
Status: CURRENT CANONICAL ATTENTION LAYER

## Purpose
Hook OS is the cross-channel first-impression, experimentation and commercial-learning layer for 222Emails.

It does not replace the Client Return System positioning, Free Revenue Recovery Check, Lead Identification Gate, proof rules, human authority, sender controls, Visual OS, Retention School architecture, Tally routing, pricing or commercial hierarchy.

## Governing sequence
ATTENTION -> RECOGNITION -> RELEVANCE -> USEFUL VALUE -> TRUST -> DIAGNOSIS -> NEXT STEP -> PAID PROGRESSION -> PROOF

## Construction rule
A strong first impression normally combines:
1. buyer recognition or a relevant condition/callout; and
2. an implication that continuing will provide useful value.

Approved families include identity/label, condition, question, diagnostic, problem/loss, outcome/opportunity, contrarian belief shift, observation, contrast/before-after, verified proof/evidence, verified specificity/number, real warning and truthful curiosity.

Specificity should be high enough for the right buyer to recognise relevance without unnecessarily excluding suitable buyers.

## First-impression map
- Cold email: sender context, subject, preview and first sentence.
- LinkedIn post: first visible line and first visible asset.
- DM: opening line and immediately visible asset.
- Call: first 5-15 seconds and first diagnostic question.
- Carousel: cover phrase and dominant visual/typographic idea.
- Video/VSL: opening scene, opening spoken line and first commercial tension.
- One-pager/audit: dominant headline and first semantic scene/evidence.
- Landing page: hero headline, supporting visual and first CTA context.
- Sales asset: opening commercial diagnosis/outcome frame.
- Retention School: lesson title/opening and first teaching promise.
- Paid ads: creative and headline/opening copy.

## Pre-test QA
Score each candidate out of 100:
- ICP relevance: 20
- Specificity: 15
- Implied value: 15
- Truth and proof safety: 15
- Clarity: 10
- Commercial tension/curiosity: 10
- Channel fit: 10
- Distinctiveness: 5

The score prioritises tests. It does not certify a winner.

Hard gates always outrank the score: truth, proof, ICP, channel rules, APEX, Visual OS, Lead Identification Gate and human authority.

## Experiment doctrine
- Explore materially different hook families before declaring a winner.
- Hold offer, body, CTA, cohort, timing and channel conditions reasonably comparable where practical.
- Define the eligible-exposure denominator and commercially qualified outcome before testing.
- Tiny, biased or contaminated samples are INSUFFICIENT DATA.
- Do not increase outreach volume merely to create a test. Use existing approved capacity.
- Live sends and publishing remain owner-approved.
- A winner in one channel is only a candidate in another channel and requires fresh QA.
- Reuse the underlying winning idea rather than mechanically copying the exact sentence.

## Winner hierarchy
Use the deepest defensible commercial signal available:
1. attributable revenue or buyer progression;
2. paid-offer or paid-diagnostic progression;
3. qualified Free Revenue Recovery Check;
4. qualified commercial conversation;
5. relevant positive response/action;
6. raw attention signal.

Views, opens, clicks and replies are diagnostic signals, not automatic commercial wins.

## Portfolio after evidence
Once genuine evidence exists, use approximately:
- 70% proven winners or close variants;
- 20% permutations of winners;
- 10% genuinely new challengers.

This is an operating heuristic, not a statistical law.

## Hook Bank states
EXPLORE, PROMOTE, CORE, RETIRE, INSUFFICIENT_DATA, BLOCKED.

Minimum experiment fields include hook ID, state, family, exact verbal hook, non-verbal hook where relevant, buyer state, vertical, channel, parent/variant lineage, experiment/cohort key, evidence/claim state, eligible exposures, relevant responses, qualified conversations, qualified Revenue Recovery Checks, paid progression, buyers, attributable revenue, Qualified Hook Yield, last-tested date, decision and uncertainty notes.

## Qualified Hook Yield
Qualified Hook Yield means commercially qualified outcomes per eligible exposure. The numerator and denominator must be defined before the experiment. Post-hoc switching is prohibited.

## Visual rule
When a visual exists, QA both the first verbal hook and the first non-verbal hook. Semantic photography, legibility, real V3 logo usage and all existing Visual OS rules remain mandatory.

## Copyright and learning boundary
Restricted or supplied third-party playbooks may inform high-level principles and internal operating logic only. Do not reproduce substantial copyrighted text, copy proprietary swipe wording, imitate distinctive creator assets or publish restricted material. 222Emails implementations must be original.

## Human authority
Hook OS does not authorise uncontrolled sends or publishing, paid spend, pricing changes, public-claim changes, DNS changes, legal/contact-route changes, scope changes or client promises.

## Non-regression lock
Hook OS leaves unchanged: brand naming, Client Return System formal naming, UK priority, ICP hierarchy, offer ladder and prices, Tally routes, Lead Identification Gate, proof rules, sender/suppression controls, channel ceilings, planned outreach volume, Retention School role, Visual OS/V3 logo rules, configured-not-equal-working doctrine and two-way-door execution doctrine.

## Cash-first loop
CREATE -> QUALIFY -> SHIP THROUGH APPROVED CAPACITY -> OBSERVE -> RECORD -> RANK -> PERMUTE -> RETEST -> SCALE WINNERS
