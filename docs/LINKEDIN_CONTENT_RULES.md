# Triple Two Emails LinkedIn content rules

## Brand naming

- Public-facing brand name: **Triple Two Emails**.
- Do not use founder-origin filler such as “this is why I made 222 Emails”, “this is why I built 222 Emails”, “this is why I started 222 Emails”, or equivalent phrasing.
- When referring to the company from Chelston’s personal LinkedIn, prefer a native LinkedIn Page mention of **Triple Two Emails** when the publishing route has verified annotation metadata.
- Never fake a native mention by assuming plain `@Triple Two Emails` will become clickable. If native annotation metadata is unavailable, use plain **Triple Two Emails** instead.
- On posts published by the Triple Two Emails Page itself, do not awkwardly self-tag the Page unless there is a specific editorial reason.

## Voice

- UK English.
- No em dashes.
- Outcome first: retention, repeat revenue, client return systems, missed-revenue recovery and lifecycle revenue.
- Avoid generic agency-origin stories unless the founder story itself is the subject of the post.
- Do not invent metrics, customer results, testimonials or certainty.

## Publishing safety

- Content reservoir or QA-ready status is not publishing permission.
- Only the owner-approved GitHub publishing gate may release content to Buffer.
- Native LinkedIn mentions must use verified LinkedIn entity metadata when sent through the Buffer API.
