## Promotion contract

A concept may move from this bank into an active `qa-replenishment-*.json` record only after:

1. 14-day textual similarity and category-fatigue check.
2. Destination-role fit against `distribution-policy.json`.
3. Truth/evidence-state review. No invented revenue, testimonials or appointment-led client outcomes.
4. Final copy QA in UK English with no em dashes.
5. Exact visual/source lock where media is used.
6. Live Buffer capacity read.
7. Explicit repository-owner approval issue.
8. Independent publication verification before analytics can teach the model.

The queue should be filled only to the configured runway. Inventory size is never a reason to publish weak or repetitive content.

## What gets killed

Do not promote generic newsletter advice, unsupported statistics, fake dashboards, fabricated client proof, duplicated personal/company copy, weak “just checking in” content, or near-duplicates of a topic already saturated in the previous 14 days. A bank gap is preferable to a quality regression.
