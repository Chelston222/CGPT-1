## 6. Proof moat: the current biggest competitive weakness

Competitors beat 222Emails today primarily on publishable proof, not strategic clarity.

### Minimum proof programme

Target:
- three paid appointment-led engagements;
- at least one completed £997 Sprint;
- three permission-cleared appointment-led proof assets;
- at least one verified on-time implementation case;
- event-backed metrics where available;
- documented sender/compliance/QA behaviour.

### Proof capture contract from day one

For every client capture:
- baseline state and date;
- exact scope installed;
- launch date;
- dependency/access delays separately;
- number eligible for journey where appropriate;
- messages / workflows actually activated;
- replies, bookings or other outcome events only where traceable;
- attribution boundary;
- client permission level: named / anonymised / private;
- client quote only after explicit approval.

### Case-study template

1. Business context.
2. What was leaking.
3. Evidence at baseline.
4. What 222Emails changed.
5. What did not change.
6. Implementation timeline.
7. Verified outputs.
8. Verified commercial outcomes.
9. Attribution limitations.
10. Client voice if approved.
11. System screenshot / map with sensitive data removed.
12. CTA to Free Revenue Recovery Check.

Never manufacture “typical results” from competitors' data.

## 8. Zero-cost risk reversal

Do not copy performance guarantees that would create cash risk before the economics are proven.

Use safer certainty:

- **Free diagnostic:** no obligation.
- **Not-fit promise:** if deeper work is not justified, say so.
- **Scope certainty:** written deliverables before the £997 Sprint begins.
- **Build-quality guarantee:** defects inside agreed deliverables fixed before sign-off, subject to third-party/platform limitations.
- **Handover certainty:** documentation and ownership supplied so the client is not trapped.

Consider stronger financial risk reversal only after enough paid evidence exists to model expected delivery cost and failure rate.
