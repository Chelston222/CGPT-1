# 222Emails LinkedIn content strategy — 2026

## Current operating thesis

The feed should be visual enough to stop and teach, but not so visual that every idea is forced into an asset. Strong native text remains a deliberate format, not a fallback.

Use a rolling **70% visual / 30% text-only** test as the default starting point. Across each ten strong feed posts, aim for:

- 3 PDF document/carousel posts for frameworks, comparisons, teardown logic or multi-step education
- 2 believable real-world/photo-led posts where the human context strengthens the lesson
- 2 proof or educational single-image visuals
- 3 deliberately text-only posts for punchy founder POV, diagnosis or one-idea teaching

Do not force a visual where it weakens the post. Visuals must obey 222Emails brand and evidence rules, use UK English and GBP where money is shown, avoid fabricated proof and avoid collage unless a separate composite asset is explicitly requested.

## Recommended six-week pillar mix

- 30% email and revenue-recovery education: client-return journeys, segmentation, timing, conversion, deliverability and retention.
- 20% proof and customer outcomes: honest before/after systems, experiments, evidence and limits. No invented results.
- 20% founder/operator point of view: Chelston's decisions, lessons, mistakes and defensible beliefs.
- 15% buyer diagnostics: symptoms, cost of inaction, objections and self-assessments.
- 10% market research/commentary: translate buyer, CRM and email shifts into a practical action.
- 5% offer/community: a clear 222Emails route or relevant company update.

## Channel jobs

- **Personal profile:** founder-led, human, diagnostic and conversational. Keep first-person operator judgement and real-world context here.
- **Main 222Emails Page:** buyer-facing authority, clear commercial diagnosis, useful frameworks and proof-safe offers.
- **222Emails | Retention Lab:** deeper education, models, lifecycle frameworks and specialist explanations.

A shared core idea may travel across channels, but exact duplicate distribution should be the exception. Rewrite the hook, framing and CTA for the identity publishing it. Stagger reused ideas when possible.

## Frequency starting ranges

Treat frequency as an experiment, not a quota:

- Personal: approximately 10–14 strong posts/week
- Main 222Emails Page: approximately 7–10/week
- Retention Lab: approximately 5–7/week

Scale only when per-post reach, target-role engagement and commercial response remain healthy. Current Buffer research shows higher posting bands can outperform low frequency, but its top bucket is 11+ posts/week rather than proof that five posts per day per channel is optimal.

## Timing policy for new, unapproved inventory

Do **not** silently move an already owner-approved schedule. Timing changes require a new revision and approval.

For new content, use the following starting distribution in Europe/London time:

- about 70% of priority posts between **15:00 and 20:00**
- about 20% around lunch/midday as a control group
- about 10% in the traditional morning window

Bias the strongest assets towards Wednesday to Friday while preserving enough morning/midday observations to compare against our own account data. Buffer's July 2026 analysis of 4.8 million LinkedIn posts found the general weekday peak shifted later, with 15:00–20:00 strongest overall and Wednesday to Friday particularly strong. Account-specific results outrank generic benchmarks once enough observations exist.

## Quality gate

Every post must do at least one job: teach something useful, prove an outcome, reframe a buyer problem or reveal credible operator judgement. Prefer named assumptions, concrete guidance and honest evidence. Use UK English, plain language, no hashtags or emojis by default and no fabricated customer proof.

A carousel must earn its slot because the sequence is easier to understand visually. A real photo must add useful context rather than merely decorate the post. A single-image visual must communicate one clear idea quickly. Text-only posts should remain concise and native to LinkedIn.

## Measurement hierarchy

Commercial metrics outrank vanity reach. Review performance by channel, format and time-window cohort using:

1. Revenue Recovery Check starts/completions, qualified enquiries and attributable opportunities/revenue where defensible
2. Relevant DMs, target-role comments and relevant profile/Page visits
3. Saves, clicks and other intent signals where available
4. Impressions and generic engagement as diagnostic context

Buffer post metrics may lag the source network by roughly a day. PDF/document posts also retain a native LinkedIn analytics check because Buffer's LinkedIn PDF-carousel analytics can be incomplete or unavailable.

Review cohorts after enough observations rather than reacting to one post. Change the pillar, format or timing mix by no more than ten percentage points in one review cycle unless there is a clear operational failure.
