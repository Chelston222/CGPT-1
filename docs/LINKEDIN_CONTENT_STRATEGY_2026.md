# 222 Emails LinkedIn content strategy — 2026

## Recommended six-week mix

- 30% email revenue education: client-return journeys, segmentation, timing, conversion, deliverability and retention.
- 20% proof and customer outcomes: honest before/after systems, experiments, evidence and limits. No invented results.
- 20% founder/operator point of view: Chelston's decisions, lessons, mistakes and defensible beliefs.
- 15% buyer diagnostics: symptoms, cost of inaction, objections and self-assessments.
- 10% market research/commentary: translate buyer, CRM and email shifts into a practical action.
- 5% offer/community: a clear 222 Emails route or relevant company update.

Use approximately four text posts, two document/carousel posts, two proof visuals and two short native videos per ten posts. Add one deeper LinkedIn article or newsletter edition each month and atomise it into feed posts.

## Channel jobs

- **Personal — 3 posts/week:** first-person operator judgement and relationships. Target mix: 40% founder POV, 25% practical advice, 20% proof/lessons, 10% conversation, 5% offer.
- **Main 222 Emails page — 2 posts/week:** durable frameworks and evidence. Target mix: 35% practical frameworks, 30% proof/case studies, 20% research/diagnostics, 10% services, 5% company/community.
- **Secondary page — 1 post/week initially:** one niche audience promise, not duplicate distribution. Increase only if its own results justify it.

Reserve combinations for flagship research, a substantial case study, an event or a genuinely reusable resource. Rewrite the hook for each identity and stagger publication by 24–72 hours.

## Quality gate

Every post must do at least one job: teach something useful, prove an outcome, reframe a buyer problem or reveal credible operator judgement. Prefer named assumptions, concrete guidance and honest evidence. Use UK English, plain language, no hashtags or emojis by default and no fabricated customer proof.

Review performance after six weeks (approximately 36 slots) using qualified comments and DMs, saves, target-role engagement, profile/page views, enquiries and influenced opportunities. Change the pillar mix by no more than ten percentage points in one review cycle.
