# 222 Emails — Lifecycle Revenue Map

## Purpose

A flagship authority asset showing how 222 Emails thinks about ecommerce lifecycle revenue as a connected system rather than isolated sends.

## Core positioning

**Find the leak. Build the system. Recover, generate and protect more revenue.**

## Map architecture

### CONVERT
**Welcome**
- Common leak: subscribers receive weak or generic onboarding
- System: structured welcome sequence
- Commercial job: turn new subscribers into first-time buyers

**Browse**
- Common leak: product interest disappears with no follow-up
- System: browse abandonment
- Commercial job: convert product interest into purchase momentum

### RECOVER
**Cart**
- Common leak: high-intent carts are left unfinished
- System: cart recovery
- Commercial job: recover purchases that nearly happened

**Checkout**
- Common leak: payment, delivery, trust or friction blocks completion
- System: checkout recovery
- Commercial job: recover people who were already trying to pay

### GROW
**Post-purchase**
- Common leak: first-order trust is not developed
- System: education, usage and follow-up
- Commercial job: increase customer value after purchase #1

**Cross-sell**
- Common leak: no relevant next-product recommendation
- System: contextual cross-sell
- Commercial job: increase order frequency and customer value

**Replenishment**
- Common leak: customers forget to reorder consumable products
- System: timed replenishment reminders
- Commercial job: capture predictable repeat demand

### RETAIN
**Repeat Purchase**
- Common leak: returning is harder than it needs to be
- System: refill, recommendation and reorder journey
- Commercial job: create purchase #2

**Win-back**
- Common leak: dormant customers quietly disappear
- System: reactivation sequence
- Commercial job: recover existing customer demand

**VIP**
- Common leak: best customers receive the same treatment as everyone else
- System: VIP segmentation, early access and relevant offers
- Commercial job: protect and grow highest-value customers
