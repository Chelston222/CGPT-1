## Qualification

### Priority A
- Ecommerce brand already generating revenue
- Clear product-market fit
- Existing traffic/customer base
- Weak or incomplete lifecycle automation
- Decision-maker is reachable
- Klaviyo or comparable email platform already in place, or willing to implement

### Priority B
- Early-stage ecommerce brand with meaningful traffic
- Strong offer but weak retention/recovery infrastructure
- Needs clear prioritisation before implementation

### Low priority
- No live store
- No meaningful traffic or customer volume
- Requests broad free strategy unrelated to lifecycle email
- No authority or intent to implement
