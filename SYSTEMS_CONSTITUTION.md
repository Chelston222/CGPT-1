## Hook Operating System
Hook OS is an additive attention, experimentation and commercial-learning layer.

Sequence:
`ATTENTION -> RECOGNITION -> RELEVANCE -> USEFUL VALUE -> TRUST -> DIAGNOSIS -> NEXT STEP -> PAID PROGRESSION -> PROOF`

Every customer-facing asset has a first impression. Where relevant, audit both verbal and non-verbal hooks.

Hook performance is ranked by the deepest defensible commercial signal available, not by reach alone. Raw views, opens, clicks and replies are diagnostic signals.

Testing rules:
- explore materially different hook families first;
- define the eligible-exposure denominator and qualified outcome before the test;
- hold other meaningful conditions reasonably comparable where practical;
- tiny or contaminated samples are INSUFFICIENT DATA;
- do not increase outreach volume merely to test;
- once evidence exists, roughly 70% proven/close variants, 20% winner permutations and 10% new challengers may be used as a heuristic;
- a winner in one channel is only a candidate in another and requires fresh QA;
- live sends and publishing remain owner-approved.
