CLINIC-DEFINED TIMING

The clinic decides the appropriate treatment journey. 222Emails builds the commercial follow-up around it.

Treatment suitability, clinical intervals and patient-specific decisions belong to qualified clinic professionals. 222Emails does not invent clinical timing or give medical advice. The return system uses the clinic's approved rules, consent model and operational process.

EnquiredDefine useful follow-up without assuming suitability.

COMPLIANCE BY DESIGN

A retention system is only useful if it respects consent, eligibility and clinical boundaries.

What the system should know

  • Whether marketing communication is permitted
  • Whether the customer state is appropriate for the journey
  • Which clinic-approved timing rule applies
  • When a reply requires human judgement
  • What event should stop the automation

What automation should never pretend to know

  • Whether a treatment is clinically suitable
  • Individual medical advice or diagnosis
  • A treatment interval the clinic has not approved
  • Whether a sensitive question can safely be resolved by a generic message
  • Commercial outcomes that the available data cannot prove

THE COMPETITIVE DIFFERENCE

Reactivation is one part of the client journey, not the whole system.

A one-off database campaign can create activity. A Client Return System also defines what happens to new enquiries, consultations, cancellations, due-back clients, replies, bookings and staff handoffs so the business does not have to restart from zero each month.

AESTHETICS QUESTIONS

Clear boundaries before automation.

Do you decide when clients should have another treatment?

No. Clinical suitability and treatment timing must come from the clinic and its qualified professionals. 222Emails builds communication and workflow around approved rules.

Can you reactivate our old database?

Potentially, but eligibility, consent, suppression and relevance need to be established first. A list is not automatically a permission to market to everyone on it.

Do we need to replace our clinic software?

Not by default. Existing booking and CRM tools are preferred wherever they can support the required data, triggers and actions reliably.

Can this reduce manual chasing?

That is one of the intended operating benefits where the workflow is suitable for automation. Human handoffs remain explicit where judgement is required.
