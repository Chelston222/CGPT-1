WHAT THIS TOOL DOES NOT TELL YOU

Opportunity size is not the same thing as recoverable revenue.

Eligibility

Some past customers should not be contacted or may not be suitable for the same journey. Permission and relevance matter.

Timing

A customer can be inactive without being due back. The right return window depends on the business and service.

Mechanism

The actual leak may be enquiry follow-up, cancellation recovery, poor data, missing ownership or something else entirely.
