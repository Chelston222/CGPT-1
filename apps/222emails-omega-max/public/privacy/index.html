Privacy

How 222Emails collects and uses personal data through this website.

Last updated: 16 August 2026. This notice explains how 222Emails handles personal data collected through this website and direct enquiries.

Who we are

222Emails provides Revenue Recovery Systems for appointment-led businesses. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@222emails.com.

What we collect

When you use the Fit Check or contact us, we may collect your name, business name, email address, optional website or social profile, the revenue-leak category you select, and any context you choose to provide. Our hosting provider may also process ordinary technical logs needed to deliver and secure the website.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To respond to your enquiry, assess fit and provide the next-step recommendation you requested. This is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To manage genuine business enquiries, maintain records, prevent abuse and improve our service. We rely on legitimate interests where appropriate and balance those interests against your rights.
  • We do not add you to marketing simply because you submit a Fit Check. Where marketing consent is needed, it will be requested separately.

Who receives your data

We use service providers that support website hosting, form processing, email and business administration. They process data only as needed to provide those services. The website is hosted on Netlify.

International processing

Some service providers may process data outside the UK. Where this happens, we expect appropriate legal safeguards to be used where required.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only for as long as reasonably needed to deal with the enquiry, manage any resulting business relationship, maintain necessary records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. If an enquiry does not progress, we review whether it still needs to be retained.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and in some cases to data portability. You can exercise these rights by emailing us.

Your right to object: where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object to that processing. We will consider your objection in line with applicable data protection law.

Automated decisions

We do not make solely automated decisions through this website that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Complaints

Please contact us first if you have a concern. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
