WHERE INTENT CAN STALL

The provider may already have the prospect or learner. The journey can still stop between steps.

01

A warm enquiry never progresses

Someone asks about a programme but the next useful action is not clear, timely or owned.

02

An application or deposit stalls

Commercial intent exists, but an incomplete step sits unnoticed until the cohort is nearly full or starting.

03

A past learner disappears after completion

Progression, upskilling, repeat learning and referral opportunities remain invisible because the relationship is treated as finished.

SYSTEMS WE CAN SUPPORT

Follow-up around the actual learner decision path.

Enquiry follow-up

Define what happens when interest does not immediately become an application or enrolment.

Application recovery

Make incomplete applications and missing steps visible without endless chasing.

Deposit / enrolment progression

Remove genuine operational friction where the prospect still intends to join.

Pre-start assurance

Identify missing actions before the intake rather than discovering non-starts too late.

Progression journeys

Show suitable next pathways based on programme history and provider rules.

Alumni return

Segment past learners by real history and relevance instead of blasting the full database.

BOUNDARIES THAT MATTER

Follow-up should clarify genuine next steps, not manufacture urgency.

Appropriate system jobs

  • Identify stalled enquiry, application or enrolment states
  • Assign ownership and next actions
  • Prompt genuine missing operational steps
  • Escalate questions that require staff judgement
  • Stop immediately when the state changes

Provider-owned decisions

  • Admissions and learner suitability
  • Funding or eligibility decisions
  • Accreditation and qualification rules
  • Learner-support decisions
  • Employment, earnings or outcome claims
