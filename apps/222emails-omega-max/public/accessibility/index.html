Accessibility

How 222Emails approaches accessibility on this website.

Last updated: 16 August 2026. We aim to make this website usable by as many people as possible. We do not currently claim independent certification or full formal WCAG conformance.

Our approach

The site has been designed with WCAG 2.2 AA principles in mind, including keyboard focus states, responsive layouts, readable contrast, semantic headings, reduced-motion handling and accessible form labels.

Known limitations

Automated and manual checks do not guarantee that every accessibility issue has been found. The site has not yet completed an independent accessibility audit or broad assistive-technology user test.

What you should be able to do

  • Navigate the main site and form using a keyboard.
  • Zoom and use the site on narrow mobile screens without horizontal scrolling in the tested layouts.
  • Use visible focus indicators on interactive controls.
  • Reduce non-essential motion through your device preference.

Report a problem

If you cannot access information or complete the Fit Check, email hello@222emails.com. Tell us what you were trying to do and, if useful, the device or assistive technology you were using. We will try to provide the information or route in an alternative format.

Review

We will review this statement when the website changes materially and as further accessibility testing is completed.
