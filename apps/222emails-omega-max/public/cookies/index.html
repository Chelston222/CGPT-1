How cookies and similar technologies are used on the 222Emails website.

Last updated: 16 August 2026. This version of the 222Emails website is intentionally built without analytics, advertising or profiling cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies can store or read information on your device. Some are necessary for a website to operate, while others are used for analytics, advertising or personalisation.

What this site uses

222Emails does not intentionally set analytics, advertising or profiling cookies on this production build. Our hosting and security infrastructure may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms where required to deliver or protect the service.

Third-party links

If you follow a link to a third-party service, such as Calendly, that service may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Its policies apply once you visit that service.

Future changes

If we introduce non-essential analytics, advertising or other optional cookies, we will update this page and implement an appropriate consent choice before using them where required.
