WHAT 222EMAILS DOES

We look for the gap after demand already exists.

Cold enquiry follow-up

Define what should happen when someone shows intent but has not booked yet.

Rebooking

Build customer-state logic around when a useful next appointment becomes relevant.

Client reactivation

Identify genuinely lapsed customers and create an appropriate route back where consent and context allow.

No-show and cancellation recovery

Give interrupted appointments a defined next action instead of leaving them invisible.

Lifecycle communication

Use email, SMS, CRM and staff actions where they fit the journey rather than treating one channel as the strategy.

System QA and measurement

Document triggers, stop rules, handoffs, permissions and reporting so the system can be understood and improved.

THE OPERATING STANDARD

Evidence before theatre.

Founder-led

The work is not sold as a large agency operation and then handed to an invented or hidden team.

Existing tools first

222Emails prefers to use software already in the business when it can do the required job properly.

Claims stay inside the evidence

Verified, estimated, illustrative and unknown information should remain distinguishable. Capability is not passed off as client ROI.
