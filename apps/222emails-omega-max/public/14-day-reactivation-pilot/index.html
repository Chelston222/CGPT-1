GOOD FIT

Usually suitable when…

  • You have a usable pool of previous clients or past enquiries
  • There is a lawful and appropriate route to contact the selected people
  • The business can handle replies and fulfil new bookings or next steps
  • There is one commercially sensible segment worth testing
  • You are willing to measure what happened rather than assume success

NOT A SHORTCUT

Usually not suitable when…

  • The underlying service experience is the real problem
  • The database is unusable, unverified or lacks an appropriate contact basis
  • Open complaints or sensitive cases need human resolution first
  • There is no viable booking or fulfilment capacity
  • The expectation is guaranteed revenue from a mass blast
