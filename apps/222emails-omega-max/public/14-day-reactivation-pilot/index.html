WHO MAY FIT

The opportunity has to exist before the Pilot deserves to run.

Useful signals

  • Past customers with a legitimate reason to return
  • Past enquiries that did not complete a booking

  • Customers who did not rebook when a next step would normally make sense

COMMON QUESTIONS

The Pilot in plain English.

Why is the Pilot £0?

It is a controlled acquisition and validation route for suitable businesses. The purpose is to test a real reactivation opportunity before either side assumes a larger system is justified.

Will I be charged after 14 days?

No automatic charge is created. If the evidence supports keeping or expanding the system, paid options can be discussed separately.

Do you guarantee bookings or revenue?

No. The Pilot measures real behaviour. Replies, appointments and confirmed revenue are recorded only where evidence supports them.

What if my retention problem is broader than reactivation?

The £197 Revenue Recovery Check is the deeper diagnostic route when the leakage is broader or unclear. A £995 implementation build can be prescribed later where fit and written scope justify it.

Do you need medical or sensitive records?

No medical records, clinical notes or treatment histories are needed for a standard Pilot. We use the minimum data needed for the agreed commercial journey.
