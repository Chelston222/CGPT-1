Privacy notice

This private 222Emails integration receives transaction and subscription events from Payhip for internal order operations, reporting and customer support. It stores the minimum operational fields needed for those purposes, including transaction identifiers, product details and buyer email where supplied. Payhip IP address fields are deliberately not retained.

Access to reporting and control endpoints is protected by a secret bearer token. Payhip API credentials are stored as hosting environment secrets and are never returned by this service.

Data should be retained only for as long as required for legitimate business, accounting, support and legal purposes. Requests about personal data should be handled through the 222Emails business contact route.
