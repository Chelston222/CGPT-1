# 222 Emails Facebook Groups Acquisition OS

## Mission
Turn relevant Facebook groups into a repeatable source of qualified UK owner conversations and Revenue Leak Audit opportunities without spam, browser automation, or dependency on Facebook's retired Groups publishing API.

## Non-negotiables
1. GitHub controls planning, QA, tracking and reminders.
2. The final Facebook group post is always made by Chelston manually.
3. Check the group's live rules immediately before every post.
4. Never paste identical copy into multiple groups back-to-back.
5. No cold DM to group members solely because they are members. DM only after a relevant public interaction, explicit invitation, or a clearly legitimate prospecting basis.
6. Direct promotion is reserved for groups where current rules permit it. In owner/vertical communities use authority-first diagnostics.
7. Every lead is moved into the existing 222 Emails audit pipeline rather than creating a parallel sales process.

## 30-day operating cadence
- Days 1-7: establish presence, test hooks and validate group rules.
- Days 8-14: double down on groups producing comments/DMs and start mini-audit threads.
- Days 15-21: add proof-led and objection-led posts, retire weak groups.
- Days 22-30: concentrate 70% of effort into the highest lead-yield groups and convert audit requests to implementation opportunities.

### Daily volume
- 3 primary group posts per day maximum.
- Up to 2 authority comments on existing threads where Chelston can genuinely help.
- Reply to every meaningful comment the same day where practical.
- Follow up only with people who engaged or asked for help.

## Comment scripts
### Someone comments the CTA keyword
Thanks [Name]. Send me your website/store URL by DM and I’ll take a look. I’ll focus on the highest-impact customer-journey leak I can identify first, then tell you what I would prioritise.

### Someone asks what the audit includes
I look at the journey from first visit/enquiry through conversion, onboarding, repeat purchase and reactivation. The goal is not to hand you a generic email checklist. It is to identify where people are currently dropping out and what follow-up system could realistically recover more of that revenue.

### Someone says they already use email marketing
That is usually where the interesting part starts. Having email running and having the right lifecycle system are different things. I’d look at what is missing, where sequencing is weak, whether customer behaviour changes the messaging, and where existing traffic/customers are not being followed up properly.

### Price question in public
The audit itself is free. If there is a worthwhile gap and you want 222 Emails to implement it afterwards, I would scope that separately. There is no obligation to buy anything from the audit.

## Lead stages
NEW_ENGAGEMENT -> QUALIFIED -> URL_RECEIVED -> AUDIT_QUEUED -> AUDIT_DELIVERED -> IMPLEMENTATION_OFFERED -> WON / NURTURE / LOST

## Qualification
Prioritise leads with existing traffic, customers, enquiries, bookings or an active ecommerce store. Strong signals include meaningful order volume, paid acquisition, repeat-purchase potential, a customer list, obvious lifecycle gaps and a decision-maker engaging directly.

## Audit handoff
For every qualified lead create or update the existing 222 Emails audit record with:
- source = Facebook Group
- group name
- post archetype
- CTA keyword
- prospect/business
- URL
- problem observed
- likely lifecycle opportunity
- audit status
- next action
- follow-up date
- eventual revenue outcome

## KPI framework
### Activity guardrails
- 3 high-quality group posts/day maximum
- 0 unapproved-rule posts
- 0 identical mass-post bursts
- 100% meaningful comments answered
- 100% requested audits logged

### Week 1 targets
- 15-20 compliant posts
- 10+ meaningful owner conversations
- 5+ audit requests
- 3+ qualified audits

### Week 2 targets
- 15-20 compliant posts
- 15+ meaningful owner conversations
- 7+ audit requests
- 5+ qualified audits
- 2+ implementation conversations

### Weeks 3-4 optimisation targets
Primary metric: qualified audit requests per 10 posts.
Secondary metrics: comment-to-DM rate, DM-to-URL rate, URL-to-audit rate, audit-to-offer rate, offer-to-win rate, revenue per group, revenue per post archetype.

Do not optimise for impressions alone. A smaller group producing owners with real revenue leakage is more valuable than a huge promotion group producing reactions from other service providers.

## Kill / scale rules
- SCALE: 2+ qualified leads from a group within 10 posts, or any won client attributable to the group.
- WATCH: engagement but no qualified lead after 5 posts.
- RETIRE: 10 compliant posts with no qualified conversations, repeated moderation friction, or audience dominated by sellers rather than buyers.
- PAUSE immediately if admins warn against promotion or posts are repeatedly removed.

## Omega review every 7 days
Score each group and archetype on:
1. ICP density
2. rule compatibility
3. meaningful comments
4. qualified leads
5. audits requested
6. offers created
7. revenue won
8. time cost
9. moderation risk
10. repeatability

Reallocate the next week's posting slots toward the top quartile. Never increase volume to compensate for weak relevance.
