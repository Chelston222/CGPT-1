Businesses spend a lot of energy trying to win the next customer.

More content. More ads. More offers. More followers. More leads.

Meanwhile, there can be customers sitting in the booking system who already know the business.

They have already crossed the hardest bridge: they trusted you once.

The question is not only “How do we acquire more?”

It is also: what is systematically bringing the right people back?

Not every past customer is recoverable, and not every lapsed customer should be contacted.

But before spending another pound acquiring fresh demand, I would want to know how many previous customers are contactable, when they naturally need the service again, what happens after cancellations or no-shows, and whether anything identifies those return moments automatically.

Sometimes the next growth lever is not more demand.

It is getting more value from demand you already paid to create.
