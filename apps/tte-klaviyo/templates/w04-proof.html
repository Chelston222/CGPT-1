CLIENT RETURN SYSTEM

It is not “send more emails”.

It is deciding which customer moment deserves which next action.

A useful system starts with the journey, not the channel.

ENQUIRY
Did genuine interest receive the right response and next step?
ATTENDANCE
What happens after a cancellation, no-show or completed appointment?
RETURN WINDOW
When is the customer naturally likely to need the service again?
WIN-BACK
Who has quietly drifted away, and is there a relevant reason to reconnect?

Email, SMS, CRM automation or a staff task can all be mechanisms. The commercial job comes first.

That is why a Client Return System should be judged by qualified customer progression and attributable commercial outcomes, not by how many messages it sends.
