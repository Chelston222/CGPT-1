THE APPOINTMENT CLIFF

The journey should not end when the appointment does.

Most appointment-led businesses have a clear journey before the appointment: enquiry, booking, reminder, attendance.

Then the customer leaves and the system often goes quiet.

That gap is what I call the Appointment Cliff: the point where returning starts depending more on the customer remembering you than on a deliberate return journey.

A better return journey can include:
a useful post-appointment follow-up, a rebooking prompt near the natural return window, a review request when the experience is fresh, and a win-back route if somebody quietly disappears.

The point is not to message more. It is to make the next communication relevant to where the customer actually is.

Quick diagnostic: after a happy customer leaves today, what happens next without a member of staff remembering to do it manually?

Reply with the answer if you want. It is one of the fastest ways to spot whether there is a Client Return Gap.
