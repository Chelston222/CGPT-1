# The 222 Lifecycle Revenue Engine

## Purpose
Turn permission-based attention into qualified commercial opportunities, client outcomes, proof and then more attention.

## Customer Revenue Journey
1. Attention Engine: LinkedIn, founder content, website, outreach, audits, referrals, partners and selective paid acquisition.
2. Capture: permission-based subscriber enters the Klaviyo Email List.
3. Welcome Engine: Relationship -> Problem Awareness -> Method -> Proof -> Conversion.
4. Diagnostic Engine: Free Follow-Up Fit Check identifies the first commercially meaningful follow-up gap.
5. Commercial Routing: sell the next logical step, not the entire relationship at once.
6. Client Outcomes: Revenue Recovered, Conversion Lift, Automated Revenue, Customer Retention, Lifecycle Revenue.
7. Proof Engine: case studies, before/after, revenue evidence, flow visualisations, LinkedIn proof content and referrals feed the next round of attention.
