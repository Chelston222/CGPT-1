# 222Emails Lifecycle Revenue Engine

## Purpose
Turn permission-based attention into qualified commercial opportunities, client outcomes, proof and then more attention.

## Commercial position
222Emails builds turnkey Client Return Systems for appointment-led businesses. Email, SMS and other approved lifecycle channels are implementation mechanisms. Revenue recovery is the commercial outcome and diagnostic language.

## Customer revenue journey
1. Attention: LinkedIn, founder content, website, approved outreach, referrals, partners and selective paid acquisition.
2. Capture: a permissioned subscriber enters the Klaviyo Email List.
3. Owned email: one useful commercial job per message under Email Revenue OS.
4. Diagnostic: the Free Revenue Recovery Check identifies substantiated follow-up, rebooking and return gaps.
5. Commercial routing: sell the next logical step rather than forcing the whole relationship at once.
6. Client outcomes: measure verified commercial progression and attributable results.
7. Proof: evidence feeds future content and testing without overstating causality.

## Email Revenue OS
Default reasoning sequence:

`HOOK -> LEAK -> MECHANISM -> VALUE -> RECOVERY -> NEXT STEP`

RETURN is a composition aid, not a visible formula:
- Recognition
- Economic tension
- Truth or mechanism
- Useful action
- Recovery path
- Next step

A QA score is a release gate, not market proof. Flagship material requires at least 95/100 and zero hard fails.
