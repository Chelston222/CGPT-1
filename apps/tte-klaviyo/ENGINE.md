# 222Emails Lifecycle Revenue Engine

## Purpose
Turn permission-based attention into qualified commercial opportunities, Client Return System demand, client outcomes, proof and then more attention.

## Canonical positioning
222Emails builds Turnkey Client Return Systems for appointment-led businesses. Email is a delivery mechanism inside the system, not the category we sell.

## Customer revenue journey
1. Attention: LinkedIn, founder content, Retention School, website, referrals, partners and approved outreach.
2. Capture: a permissioned subscriber enters the Klaviyo Email List.
3. Welcome: relationship -> belief shift -> diagnosis -> mechanism -> next step.
4. Diagnostic: the Free Revenue Recovery Check identifies commercially meaningful client-return gaps.
5. Commercial routing: sell the next logical step, not the entire relationship at once.
6. Client outcomes: rebooking, reactivation, missed-revenue recovery, stronger lifetime value and more repeat revenue where evidence supports it.
7. Proof: verified client outcomes, process evidence and commercial progression feed the next round of attention.

## Email Revenue OS composition
Every message defaults to one central idea. Use the sequence Hook -> Leak -> Mechanism -> Value -> Recovery -> Next Step when useful, not as a visible rigid template.

RETURN is the composition aid: Recognition, Economic tension, Truth/mechanism, Useful action, Recovery path, Next step.

Subject, preheader and first line must form one truthful opening. No bait and switch, fabricated urgency, manufactured proof or multiple competing primary CTAs.

## Canonical welcome engine
- E01 Day 0: `Welcome to 222Emails` | relationship and expectation setting | reply CTA.
- E02 Day 1: `You already paid to acquire them` | acquisition-versus-return belief shift | no primary external CTA.
- E03 Day 3: `The appointment cliff` | diagnose what happens after the appointment | soft reply CTA.
- E04 Day 5: `What a Client Return System actually does` | explain the mechanism without overclaiming results | no primary external CTA.
- E05 Day 7: `Where are your bookings slipping away?` | direct diagnostic conversion | Free Revenue Recovery Check.

## North star
Attributable revenue and qualified buyer progression. Opens and clicks are diagnostics, not the final objective.

Priority: attributable revenue/buyer progression -> paid progression -> qualified Free Revenue Recovery Check -> qualified commercial conversation -> relevant positive action -> attention signal.

## Delivery OS
Strategy & copy -> GitHub source -> static Email Revenue OS QA -> GitHub Actions -> Klaviyo API -> Klaviyo Draft -> automated live-account QA -> human QA -> controlled canary -> production.

## Launch gates
Automated gates must pass first. Human/external evidence must then confirm:
1. Desktop, mobile and dark-mode rendering.
2. Seed inbox placement, links and replies.
3. SPF, DKIM and DMARC readiness/alignment appropriate to the sending setup.
4. Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe mechanism for promotional mail, not only an unsubscribe link in the body.
5. Public capture path into the permissioned list.
6. Free Revenue Recovery Check end-to-end submission and attribution event.
7. Client/qualified-opportunity exit or suppression logic.
8. Explicit go-live authorisation.

## Governance
No real send without final QA. Production activation is separated from content build and draft deployment. Email Revenue OS does not authorise sending, increase volume or bypass existing provider, suppression, evidence or human-authority gates.
