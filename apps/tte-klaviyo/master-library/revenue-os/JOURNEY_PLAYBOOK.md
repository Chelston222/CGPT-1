# Journey Selection Playbook

Use this before choosing a layout or adding modules. Start with the revenue problem.

| Revenue job | Default starting layout | High-value modules | Primary question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome hero | Premium Editorial | mechanism, benefit_stack | What valuable outcome should a new subscriber understand first? |
| Welcome founder | Founder Human | founder_note, social_proof_quote | Why should the subscriber care that this brand exists? |
| Welcome offer | Proof First | offer_box, proof_strip | Does the incentive make the desired outcome easier to reach? |
| Welcome discovery | Commerce Product | comparison, benefit_stack | How do we reduce choice overload? |
| Welcome proof | Proof First | proof_strip, testimonial | What makes buying feel safer? |
| Abandoned cart minimal | Performance Minimal | delivery_reassurance | Can we restore the shopper's momentum with almost no friction? |
| Abandoned cart product-led | Commerce Product | benefit_stack, testimonial | Why was this product worth choosing? |
| Abandoned cart objections | Proof First | objection_faq, guarantee | What real concern is blocking purchase? |
| Abandoned cart urgency | Performance Minimal | urgency_truth | Is there a genuine reason to act now? |
| Abandoned checkout | Performance Minimal | delivery_reassurance, guarantee | What final uncertainty stopped checkout? |
| Browse abandonment | Commerce Product | benefit_stack | What originally caught attention and why does it matter? |
| Product recommendation | Commerce Product | comparison, benefit_stack | Which small set best matches the customer's need? |
| New collection | Premium Editorial | benefit_stack | Which customer use case makes the collection relevant? |
| Product launch | Premium Editorial | mechanism, proof_strip | Why does this new product solve the problem differently or better? |
| Flash sale | Performance Minimal | urgency_truth, offer_box | Is the deadline real and the value obvious? |
| Promotional offer | Proof First | offer_box, proof_strip | Is the promotion attached to a meaningful customer outcome? |
| Newsletter editorial | Premium Editorial | mechanism | Is the email useful even before the reader buys anything? |
| Education/value | Founder Human | mechanism, benefit_stack | What useful belief or behaviour should change? |
| Founder hybrid | Founder Human | founder_note | Would a human note create more trust than a designed campaign? |
| Case study | Proof First | proof_strip, testimonial, mechanism | What problem, system and verified outcome can we show? |
| Post-purchase thanks | Performance Minimal | next_steps | What removes buyer anxiety immediately after purchase? |
| Post-purchase education | Founder Human | next_steps, mechanism | What helps the customer realise value faster? |
| Cross-sell | Commerce Product | benefit_stack, comparison | What genuinely improves the product already purchased? |
| Review request | Founder Human | next_steps | Has the customer had enough time to experience the promised value? |
| Referral | Founder Human | offer_box | Why is sharing valuable to both people? |
| Win-back | Founder Human | benefit_stack, offer_box | What new relevance or value justifies re-engagement? |
| Replenishment | Commerce Product | delivery_reassurance | Is timing based on realistic usage? |
| Back in stock | Commerce Product | urgency_truth | Is availability the only thing the customer needs to know? |
| Order confirmation | Performance Minimal | next_steps, delivery_reassurance | Is the transaction clear and is the next step obvious? |
| VIP retention | Premium Editorial | vip_access, founder_note | What meaningful treatment has loyalty actually earned? |

## Selection rule

If the email cannot be described as solving one of these decision problems in one sentence, the brief is not ready for design.
